\title{Who Gets Summarized? Auditing and Repairing Evidence Coverage in Product Review Summaries}

\begin{abstract}
Generative summaries increasingly mediate how shoppers read product reviews, yet it is unclear which
reviewers' concrete experiences survive compression. We audit \nTestProducts{} Amazon products with
two open generators in both directions: whether summary claims are grounded in input reviews, and
whether human-identified important experiences in the input reach the summary
(\nGoldExperiences{} experiences over \nGoldProducts{} products, \invOverlap\% found by both annotators).
Validating the measurement itself, we find that off-the-shelf evidence judges over-attribute support
(SUPPORT precision \supPNimble--\supPNli{} against human labels, $\kappa_{\text{human}}=\kappaPair$) and that
top-10 retrieval misses supporting reviews (recall \retRecallTen{}), so we judge all pairs and report
human-calibrated rates. We then propose a training-free evidence agent that verifies, selects, writes,
and self-checks. \todo{one sentence with the main result and CI, from tab\_main}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Product reviews are a primary public source of purchase information on the Web, and platforms now
place generated summaries above them. A summary of 100--120 words must drop most of what 30 reviewers
wrote; the question is \emph{which} experiences are kept and whether the kept ones are grounded.
Prior work studies polarity calibration and minority-opinion loss in opinion summarization~\cite{poca2024,refer2025}
and fine-grained faithfulness/completeness evaluation~\cite{finesure2024}. We contribute:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A two-directional audit protocol} for review summaries: output claims are checked against
every input review, and a human inventory of input experiences measures what survives
(\nGoldExperiences{} experiences; importance $\kappa_w=\invImpKappa$).
\item \textbf{A measurement-validity finding with a correction}: automatic evidence judges and top-$K$
retrieval systematically mis-measure support in this domain; we quantify the error against human pair
labels and report human-calibrated estimates~\cite{rogan1978,angelopoulos2023ppi}.
\item \textbf{Controlled real-review interventions and an evidence agent}: holding a target experience's
evidence fixed (or removing it) using only real reviews of the same product, and a training-free
plan--write--verify agent compared against prompt and frequency-plan baselines under the same length.
\end{enumerate}
